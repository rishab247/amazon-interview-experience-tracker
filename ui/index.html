Amazon Hiring Standard

The 16 Amazon Leadership Principles & STAR Playbook

Amazon evaluates behavioral alignment just as strictly as coding syntax. Every round (including the Bar Raiser) allocates 15–30 minutes to LP questions. Your stories must follow the STAR Method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

STAR Method Blueprint
  • S - Situation: Set context in 2-3 sentences.
  • T - Task: State the problem & your specific role.
  • A - Action: 70% of response: what YOU did (use 'I', not 'We').
  • R - Result: Concrete numbers: % latency reduced, $ saved.
